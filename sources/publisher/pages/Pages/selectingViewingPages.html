Select and view pages

Selecting and viewing pages are essential operations which can be easily performed through the Pages panel.

Selecting pages

Selecting a page, or multiple pages, allows you to apply actions or properties (e.g., Apply Master) to the selection.

To select a page:
  • On the Pages panel:
    • Single page: Click on a page to select it.
    • Multiple adjacent pages: Click on a page then -click on another page to select those pages and all pages in between.
    • Multiple non-adjacent pages: Click on a page then -click on additional pages.

Selected pages have a blue border around them on the Pages panel.

You can select the left or right page of a spread, or both, depending on where you click.

Viewing pages

Making a page the current, active page shows it in the document view, and allows you to easily select and edit content on that page.

To view a page:
  • On the Pages panel, double-click on a page to make it the active page.

The active page has a gray border around it on the Pages panel.
