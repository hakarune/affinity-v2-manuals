Personas

Think of Personas as different ways of working within your app. Within Affinity Publisher, several Personas are available:

  • Publisher Persona—Accesses DTP features and layout tools.
  • Designer Persona—This is the primary Persona of Affinity Designer 2, offering additional vector drawing features and tools.
  • Photo Persona—This is the primary Persona of Affinity Photo 2, offering photo editing features and tools.

You can switch between Personas with a single click, with the workspace tools and panels changing to that Persona's way of working.

The Designer Persona and Photo Persona come directly from Affinity Designer 2 and Affinity Photo 2, respectively. As such you need to have one or both apps purchased and installed to take advantage of that Persona. The options are unavailable otherwise.

About each Persona

Let's take look at each Persona's essential features...

Publisher Persona

  • Create pages, master pages and spreads
  • Lay out your text frames
  • Add picture frames
  • Add tables
  • Draw curves and shapes
  • Create artistic and frame text
  • Add Table of contents and Index
  • Use hyperlinks and anchors
  • Publish your work

Designer Persona

  • Publisher Persona's vector toolset plus:
    • Contour Tool
    • Corner Tool
    • Node Transform Tool
    • Vector Brush Tool
    • Pencil Tool

Find out more in the StudioLink to Designer Persona topic.

Many commonly used vector tools are shared across Personas.

Photo Persona

  • Make freehand, shape, and brush-based selections
  • Add pixel brush strokes (e.g., textures)
  • Erase using pixel brushes
  • Retouch with dedicated tools

Find out more in the StudioLink to Photo Persona topic.
