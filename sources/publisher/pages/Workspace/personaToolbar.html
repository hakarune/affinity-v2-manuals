Persona Toolbar

The Persona toolbar hosts the current Personas available in Affinity Publisher.

To use the Designer Persona and/or Photo Persona you need to have Affinity Designer 2 and/or Affinity Photo 2 installed, respectively.

Settings

The following options are available from the Toolbar:

  • Publisher Persona—switch to the specialized DTP design and layout environment.
  • Designer Persona—switch to the specialized vector design and art environment.
  • Photo Persona—switch to the specialized raster selection and painting environment.

The active Persona can also be switched by accessing the top Affinity Publisher menu.

The active Persona can also be switched by accessing the top Edit menu.
