Updating cross-references

Certain changes to a document may require you to manually instruct Affinity to update cross-references' values.

Preflight will generate errors for any cross-reference that is out of date or invalid after you:

  • add or delete pages.
  • change a section's name.
  • amend publication page numbering.
  • edit paragraph text at a cross-reference's target.
  • remove an anchor that is a cross-reference's target.
  • use a Chapter Name subfield in a document whose parent book is not open on the Books panel.

Updating cross-references in books

When a document is a book chapter and contains cross-references to other book chapters, ensure the book is open on the Books panel before updating cross-references.

The Books panel's Preferences menu includes options to update cross-references: in all of the current book's chapters immediately, and when outputting (exporting or printing) books.

To update a cross-reference's value:

On the Cross-References panel:

  1. Select one or more cross-references.
  2. Select Update Cross-Reference.
To update all cross-references in a document:
  • On the Cross-References panel, select Update All Cross-References.
To update all cross-references in a book:
  • On the Books panel's Preferences menu, select Cross-References>Update Cross-References.

When exporting or printing a book, the option Cross-References>Update Cross-References Before Output (on the Books panel's Preferences menu) determines whether Affinity will automatically update cross-references' values.
