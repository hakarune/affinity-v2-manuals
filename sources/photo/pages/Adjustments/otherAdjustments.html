Other adjustments

As well as tonal and color adjustments, there are a range of other adjustments which can be applied to your photo for corrective, creative or proofing purposes.

Available adjustments include:

  • LUT—adjusts the color values of a photo based on a defined matrix.
  • Invert—reverses the color values of a photo to create a negative image.
  • Posterize—expresses a photo as blocks of solid color.
  • Soft Proof—provides a preview of a photo when output for a specific color space or device.
  • Normals—used to adjust and correct normal maps used in 3D game-art apps.
