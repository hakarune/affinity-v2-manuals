Enhance adjustment

The adjustment groups together several settings for enhancing your image's tone and color.

This adjustment grouping appears in Develop Persona on the Basic panel. Individual settings appear as their own adjustment layers or filters.

Settings

The following settings can be adjusted:

  • Contrast—controls the tonal range of the image. Drag the slider to the left to decrease the contrast between dark and light areas, drag to the right to increase it.
  • Clarity—sharpens your image by controlling local contrast at image edges.
  • Saturation—increases or decreases color saturation. Drag the slider to the left to increasingly desaturate.
  • Vibrance—controls the intensity of colors in the image.
