Lighting/tonal filters

These filters can be used to adjust the contrast and lighting of images.

Available filters include:

Filter name Description Regular filter
(destructive)		 Live filter
(non‑destructive)
Haze Removal Removes slight to extreme cases of haze affecting an image.
Lighting Add one or more simulated light sources (ambient, point, direction and spot lighting).
Shadows / Highlights Manipulate tones to reduce contrast and improve detail in shadows and highlights.
