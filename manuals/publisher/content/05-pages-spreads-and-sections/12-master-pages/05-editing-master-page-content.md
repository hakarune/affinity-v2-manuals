# Editing master page content on a publication page

Placeholder content in a text frame or picture frame that is inherited from a master page can be edited on publication pages without affecting the master page itself.

![Editing master page content](../../../assets/shared/masterpage_editcontent.jpg)
*Master page (top) with placeholder frames (brown) being edited (turquoise) on publication page (bottom).*

## About editing master page placeholder content

As opposed to editing the master page itself, which affects all publication pages to which the master page is applied, you can edit the contents of any frame on a publication page, making it unique for that page. This 'local' edit will not be made on the master page itself.

Placeholder content could be an empty text or picture frame, or frames filled with filler text or a temporary image, respectively.

> **Note:** By default, you can't transform or change the properties of a master page's picture frames and text frames themselves. Only the *frame content* can be edited on a publication page; the frame itself is locked by default. [Detaching](06-detaching-and-linking-master-pages.md) the master page lets you edit properties of frames inherited from it.

**To edit master page placeholder content:**

- On a *publication page*, type directly into a text frame or place an image into a picture frame that's inherited from an applied master.
