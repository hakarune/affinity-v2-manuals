# Select and view pages

Selecting and viewing pages are essential operations which can be easily performed through the **Pages** panel.

![Selected and active pages](../../assets/shared/selectviewpages.jpg)
*The Pages panel showing the active view (gray border; Page 2,3) and selected page (blue border; page 5).*

## Selecting pages

Selecting a page, or multiple pages, allows you to apply actions or properties (e.g., **Apply Master**) to the selection.

**To select a page:**

- On the **Pages** panel:
  - Single page: Click on a page to select it.
  - Multiple adjacent pages: Click on a page then `Shift`-click on another page to select those pages and all pages in between.
  - Multiple non-adjacent pages: Click on a page then `Cmd`-click on additional pages.

> **Note:** Selected pages have a blue border around them on the **Pages** panel.

> **Note:** You can select the left or right page of a spread, or both, depending on where you click.

## Viewing pages

Making a page the current, active page shows it in the document view, and allows you to easily select and edit content on that page.

**To view a page:**

- On the **Pages** panel, double-click on a page to make it the active page.

> **Note:** The active page has a gray border around it on the **Pages** panel.
