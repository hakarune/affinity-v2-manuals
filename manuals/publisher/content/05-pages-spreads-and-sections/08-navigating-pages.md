# Navigating pages

You can navigate a document's pages using various methods to locate the one you want.

You can use the **Pages** panel to find a page by its thumbnail, or scroll the document view.

Alternatively, use the **Page navigation bar** at the bottom left of your workspace to advance through your document one page/spread at a time, or to jump to the first or last page or a specific page number.

![Page navigation bar](../../assets/images/pagenavigationbar.png)
*Page navigation bar*

**To navigate your pages/master pages:**

Do one of the following:

- On the **Page navigation bar**, click **First Spread**, **Previous Spread**, **Next Spread** or **Last Spread**.
- On the **Page navigation bar**, click in the page number box, type a page number, and then press the  .
- On the **Pages** panel, double-click the thumbnail of the page (or master page) you want to view.

> **Tip:** Commands corresponding to the **Page navigation bar**'s functionality are available on the **Document** menu, and can be assigned keyboard shortcuts in Affinity's Settings (or Preferences).

**To switch between the current page and its master page:**

- On the **Page navigation bar**, click **Toggle Master View**.

**To switch between the current page and the previously viewed page:**

- On the **Page navigation bar**, click **Previously Viewed Page**.

The previously viewed page is the one whose page number(s) or name was last shown on the **Page navigation bar**. It may be a publication page/spread or a master page.

Affinity remembers the previously viewed page's position and zoom level in the document view.

Repeated selection of this command allows you to instantly navigate between two parts of a publication.
