# Outputting books

A book can be printed or exported, in whole or in part, via the **Books** panel's preferences menu.

Before outputting, you can check the preflight status of all chapters to quickly identify which need attention, without having to open all chapters to perform checks.

**To perform preflight checks on a book:**

1. On the **Books** panel, select the chapters to be checked.
2. (Optional) Hover the pointer over a chapter's preflight status icon for confirmation of its meaning.

If a chapter has preflight warnings or errors, double-click its entry to open the corresponding document, then use the Preflight panel as usual to resolve the problems.

**To export or print a book:**

1. From the **Books** panel's Panel Preferences menu, select **Export** or **Print**.
2. Set output options as required. For **Area** (when exporting)/**Range** (when printing), select **All Chapters**, **All Chapters as Pages**, **Selected Chapters** or **Selected Chapters as Pages**.
3. Click **Export**/**Print**.

> **Note:** In a publication with facing pages, **All Chapters** and **Selected Chapters** output each two-page spread as a single PDF page. **All Chapters as Pages** and **Selected Chapters as Pages** output each two-page spread as separate, consecutive PDF pages.
