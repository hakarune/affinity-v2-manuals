# About books

A book allows you to collate multiple Affinity Publisher documents into a single publication for editing and output purposes.

Affinity Publisher's Books panel (**Window>Books**) provides a visual overview from which you can:

- Specify Affinity Publisher (.afpub) documents to include as chapters.
- Set the order of chapters.
- Ensure continuity of page, list and note numbering.
- Synchronize styles from a designated 'style source' chapter to other chapters.
- Determine how stray pages between consecutive chapters are handled.
- Perform preflight checks on chapters.
- Export or print selected or all chapters.
