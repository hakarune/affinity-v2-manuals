# Switching Personas

When you switch to a different Persona, a unique workspace layout containing the tools and panels used by that Persona is loaded. This may also change the menus available so that everything you need is at your fingertips.

**To switch to a different Persona:**

- On the Toolbar, click the Persona button (Publisher, Designer or Photo) that you want to use.   
  
![Publisher Persona](../../../assets/shared/ui/publisher_persona_on.png) ![Designer Persona](../../../assets/shared/ui/designer_persona_on.png) ![Photo Persona](../../../assets/shared/ui/photo_persona_on.png)
- **macOS:** From the **Affinity Publisher** menu, choose a Persona you wish to switch to.
- **Windows:** In the **File** menu, select a Persona from the **Personas** option.
