# Share

**macOS:**

The Share options via email, messaging, AirDrop and social media sites make it easy to distribute your document to clients, friends, and family.

**Windows:**

Sharing options within your Affinity app are only available in the Mac version.

**macOS:**

When a sharing option is selected, your document page is exported as a JPEG and made available to the relevant app or website.

Options The following sharing options are available:  **Mail**—image is placed in the body of an email, ready for sending to contacts. **Messages**—image is placed in a new message, ready for sending to buddies. **AirDrop**—image is made available for airdropping. **Add to Photos**—image is imported into Apple's Photos app. **Add to Aperture**—image is imported into Aperture (if installed). **Set Background Image**—image is used as your background image.

To share a design:  From the **File** menu, from the **Share** submenu, select a sharing option.
