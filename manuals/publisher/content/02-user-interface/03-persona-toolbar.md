# Persona Toolbar

The Persona toolbar hosts the current Personas available in Affinity Publisher.

> **Note:** To use the Designer Persona and/or Photo Persona you need to have Affinity Designer 2 and/or Affinity Photo 2 installed, respectively.

### Settings

The following options are available from the Toolbar:

- ![Publisher Persona](../../assets/shared/ui/publisher_persona_on.png) **Publisher Persona**—switch to the specialized DTP design and layout environment.
- ![Designer Persona](../../assets/shared/ui/designer_persona_on.png) **Designer Persona**—switch to the specialized vector design and art environment.
- ![Photo Persona](../../assets/shared/ui/photo_persona_on.png) **Photo Persona**—switch to the specialized raster selection and painting environment.

> **Note:**
>
> **macOS:**
>
> The active Persona can also be switched by accessing the top **Affinity Publisher** menu.
>
> **Windows:**
>
> The active Persona can also be switched by accessing the top **Edit** menu.
