# White Balance adjustment

Remove unwanted colour casts or to give your design a warmer or cooler feel.

![Before](../../assets/shared/adjustment_base.jpg)
![After](../../assets/shared/adjustment_whitebalance.jpg)

### Settings

The following settings can be adjusted in the dialog:

- **White Balance**—controls the 'temperature' of the design. Drag the slider to the left to cool the design (by adding a blue tint), drag the slider to the right to warm the design (by adding an amber tint).
- **Tint**—tints the design either towards magenta or green.
- **Picker**—allows you to sample the page to set the white point on which the white balance will be calculated. You can sample using one of the following methods:
   - Click to sample a pixel under the cursor.
  - Drag the cursor across the image to sample all pixels under the cursor. The averaged colour from those pixels is used.
  - Press the `Alt`  and drag to sample under a rectangular marquee, again using the averaged colour of those pixels.
