# Exposure adjustment

Correct highlight and shadow detail.

![Before](../../assets/shared/adjustment_base.jpg)
![After](../../assets/shared/adjustment_exposure.jpg)

### Settings

The following settings can be adjusted in the dialog:

- **Exposure**—controls the highlight/shadow levels of the image. Drag the slider to the left to increase shadows, drag to the right to increase highlights.

> **Tip:** In general:
>
>
> - Drag the slider to the left to correct overexposure.
> - Drag the slider to the right to correct underexposure.
