# Split Toning adjustment

Tints and recolours highlights and shadows.

![Before](../../assets/shared/adjustment_base.jpg)
![After](../../assets/shared/adjustment_splittoning.jpg)

### Settings

The following settings can be adjusted:

- **Highlights Hue**—controls the colour tint of highlights.
- **Highlights Saturation**—controls the intensity of the highlight colours. Drag the slider to the left to decrease colour intensity, drag to the right to increase it.
- **Shadows Hue**—controls the colour tint of shadows.
- **Shadows Saturation**—controls the intensity of shadow colours. Drag the slider to the left to decrease colour intensity, drag to the right to increase it.
- **Balance**—controls the emphasis of the adjustment. Drag the slider to the left to emphasise highlight colour, drag to the right to emphasise shadow colour.
