# Targeting objects

Targeting allows you to decide the z-order, or nesting, of an object during creation.

By default, drawn or placed objects are positioned:

- Directly above the current selection.
- At the top of the layer, if there is no selection.

This default targeting behaviour can be modified so objects can be placed or drawn below the selection, at the top of the layer (regardless of selection), or nested inside a selection.

> **Tip:** Once placed or drawn, you can reposition or nest objects using the **Layers** panel or the Order options on the Toolbar.

**![Insert behind the selection](../../assets/shared/ui/insert_behind_selection.png)

 ![Insert at the top of the layer](../../assets/shared/ui/insert_top_of_layer.png)

 ![Insert inside the selection](../../assets/shared/ui/insert_inside_selection.png)

 To change targeting behaviour:**

- From the Toolbar, choose one of the following:
   - **Insert behind the selection**
  - **Insert at the top of the layer**
  - **Insert inside the selection**

> **Note:** To revert to default targeting ensure the active button is switched off.

> **Note:** Alternatively, you can access the default behaviour, as well as the other targeting options, from the **Layer** menu's **Insertion** submenu.
