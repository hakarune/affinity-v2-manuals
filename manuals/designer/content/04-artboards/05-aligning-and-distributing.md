# Aligning and distributing artboards

Although discrete design containers, artboards can be aligned and distributed just like any page object.

Once selected, artboards can be used in conjunction with the available [aligning](../08-object-control/10-aligning-objects.md), [distributing and spacing](../08-object-control/11-distributing-and-spacing-objects.md) commands.

**![Alignment](../../assets/shared/ui/arrange.png)

 ![Move Tool](../../assets/shared/ui/move_tool.png)

 To align, distribute and space artboards:**

1. [Select multiple artboards](03-selecting-moving-and-resizing.md).
2. Do one of the following:
   - On the Toolbar, click **Alignment**, set your options from the pop-up panel, and then click **Apply**.
  - On the **Layer** menu's **Alignment** submenu, select an alignment option.
  - Select the **Move Tool** and then, on the context toolbar, select a horizontal or vertical alignment option.
