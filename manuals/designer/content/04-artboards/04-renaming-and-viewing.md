# Renaming and viewing artboards

Artboards can be renamed and selected as targets for zooming, just like any other document objects.

Newly created artboards are given the default naming convention of 'Artboard *{n}*', while copied or duplicated artboards take on the same name as the original artboard.

Artboard names appear in the **Layers** panel as well as being located above their artboard on the pasteboard. To help you organise and identify artboards, you can rename them.

You can also zoom to artboards, for focused working, using the **Zoom to Selection** option.

**To rename an artboard:**

Do one of the following:

- Double-click on the artboard's name (above the artboard) on the pasteboard, then enter a new name and click **Rename**.
- On the **Layers** panel, double-click the selected artboard's current name, then enter a new name for the artboard.

**To zoom to an artboard:**

1. [Select an artboard](03-selecting-moving-and-resizing.md).
2. From the **View** menu, select **Zoom to Selection**.

> **Tip:** You may wish to create view points for each of your artboards to allow you to quickly switch between them. For more information on creating view points, see the [Navigator panel](../23-panels/12-navigator-panel.md) topic.
