# Pan/Scroll the document view

When your document is zoomed-in, you can work on different areas of your design by using panning.

You can use the **Navigator** panel to save the document view's current zoom level and position. Multiple view points can be saved, and you can instantly navigate between them.

**![View Tool](../../assets/shared/ui/view_tool.png)

 To pan an image with the View Tool:**

1. From the **Tools** panel on the left, click the **View Tool** (or shortcut `H`).
2. Drag the zoomed-in document in relation to its view.

> **Tip:** Temporarily activate the **View Tool** by using Hot key panning: press `Spacebar`. As soon as you let go, the tool will switch back to the previously selected tool.

> **Note:** You can also pan the document view using your mouse wheel, scroll bars, or the Navigator panel.
