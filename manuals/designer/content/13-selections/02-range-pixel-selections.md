 Range pixel selections

In the Pixel Persona, you can create a pixel selection using colour or tonal ranges from your image.

![Range-based pixel selection](../../assets/shared/range_pixel_selections.png)
*Pixel selection of red colour range.*

If you decide to create a selection based on a colour or tonal range, your image is analysed and all pixels which fall into the chosen range are included in the selection. Pixels which are not in the chosen range are excluded.

Colour and tonal range selections are available from the **Select** menu. These include:

- From the **Colour Range** submenu:
   - **Select Reds**
  - **Select Blues**
  - **Select Greens**
- From the **Tonal Range** submenu:
   - **Select Midtones**
  - **Select Shadows**
  - **Select Highlights**
