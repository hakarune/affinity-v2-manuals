# Persona Toolbar

The Persona toolbar hosts the current Personas available in Affinity Designer.

### Settings

The following options are available from the Toolbar:

- ![Designer Persona](../../assets/shared/ui/designer_persona_on.png)

   **Designer Persona**—switch to the specialised vector design and art environment.
- ![Pixel Persona](../../assets/shared/ui/pixel_persona_on.png)

   **Pixel Persona**—switch to the specialised pixel adjustment environment.
- ![Export Persona](../../assets/shared/ui/export_persona_on.png)

   **Export Persona**—switch to the specialised export environment.

> **Note:** **macOS:**
>
> The active Persona can also be switched by accessing the top **Affinity Designer** menu.
>
>
> **Windows:**
>
> The active Persona can also be switched by accessing the top **File** menu.
