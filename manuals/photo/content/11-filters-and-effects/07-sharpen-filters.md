# Sharpen filters

Sharpen filters can be used to bring into focus photos that appear slightly blurred.

Filters are applied to your image from the **Filters** menu (destructive) or **Layer** menu (non-destructive) and mostly include customizable settings alongside general options. Non-destructive filters are called live filters, and, once applied, they can be identified as both a filter and a specific filter type by using unique symbols.

| Filter name | Description | Regular filter    (destructive) | Live filter    (non‑destructive) with layer symbol |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Clarity](09-shadows-highlights/01-filter-clarity.md) | Sharpens the image by affecting local contrast. | ![check](../../assets/shared/check.png) | ![check](../../assets/shared/check.png) ![Clarity live filter type](../../assets/shared/ui/clarity_live_filter.png) |
| [High Pass](07-sharpen-filters/01-filter-highpass.md) | Sharpens image edges. | ![check](../../assets/shared/check.png) | ![check](../../assets/shared/check.png) ![High pass live filter type](../../assets/shared/ui/high_pass_live_filter.png) |
| [Unsharp Mask](07-sharpen-filters/02-filter-unsharpmask.md) | Sharpens image edges as an alternative to High Pass. | ![check](../../assets/shared/check.png) | ![check](../../assets/shared/check.png) ![Unsharp mask live filter type](../../assets/shared/ui/Unsharp_mask_live_filter.png) |
