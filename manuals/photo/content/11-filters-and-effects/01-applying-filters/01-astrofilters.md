# Astrophotography filters

Astrophotography filters are used in post-processing output from the Astrophotography Stack Persona.

Available filters include:

| Filter name | Description | Regular filter    (destructive) | Live filter    (non‑destructive) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Remove Background | Eliminates background light that has become exaggerated by tone-stretching. | ![check](../../../assets/shared/check.png) |  |
