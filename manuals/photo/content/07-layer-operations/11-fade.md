# Fade

Change the strength of an effect immediately after applying it.

![Before](../../assets/shared/fade_before.jpg)
![After](../../assets/shared/fade_after.jpg)
*Using Fade option to reduce the strength of the tilt shift effect.*

The Fade option becomes active immediately after applying one of the following:

- A destructive filter e.g. Motion Blur.
- Painting.
- Erasing.
- Adjustments made in Liquify Persona.

Apart from using the **Opacity** slider in the dialog, you can also select a **Blend Mode** to manipulate how the effect interacts with layer pixels.

> **Note:** For painting, **Fade** will only affect the last stroke made.

**To use Fade:**

1. Apply one of the edits listed above.
2. Do one of the following:
  - From the top menu, select **Layer>Fade (operation name)**.
  - **macOS:** On your keyboard, press `Shift` + `Cmd` + `F`.
  - **Windows:** On your keyboard, press `Shift`+`Cmd`+`F`.
3. Adjust the Opacity and Blend Mode in the dialog, as required.
4. Click **Apply**.
