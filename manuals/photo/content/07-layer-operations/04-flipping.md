# Flipping

Layer content can be flipped around its horizontal or vertical center.

![Before](../../assets/shared/transform_flip_before.jpg)
![After](../../assets/shared/transform_flip_after.jpg)
*Flipping an image horizontally*

**To flip or rotate layer contents:**

1. Select one or more layers.
2. From the **Arrange** menu, select **Flip Horizontal** or **Flip Vertical**.

If multiple layers are selected, they are flipped around the horizontal or vertical center of the entire selection, not their individual centers.
