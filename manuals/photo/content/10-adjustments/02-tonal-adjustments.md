# Tonal adjustments

Tonal adjustments are used to correct and modify the lightness values within photos.

Available tonal adjustments include:

- [Brightness and Contrast](02-tonal-adjustments/01-adjustment-brightnesscontrast.md)—uses two settings to adjust the lightness of all pixels within a photo and the tonal differences between them, respectively.
- [Curves](02-tonal-adjustments/02-adjustment-curves.md)—uses graph manipulation to precisely adjust the lightness and contrast of a photo.
- [Exposure](02-tonal-adjustments/03-adjustment-exposure.md)—retrieves lost detail in highlights or shadows caused by poor exposure.
- [Levels](02-tonal-adjustments/04-adjustment-levels.md)—reassigns the black, white and gamma points within a photo to redistribute pixel lightness.
- [Shadows / Highlights](02-tonal-adjustments/05-adjustment-shadowshighlights.md)—applies tonal adjustment to the darkest and/or lightest areas in a photo.
