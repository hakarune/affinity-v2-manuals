# Other adjustments

As well as [tonal](02-tonal-adjustments.md) and [color](03-color-adjustments.md) adjustments, there are a range of other adjustments which can be applied to your photo for corrective, creative or proofing purposes.

Available adjustments include:

- [LUT](04-other-adjustments/01-adjustment-3dlut.md)—adjusts the color values of a photo based on a defined matrix.
- [Invert](04-other-adjustments/02-adjustment-invert.md)—reverses the color values of a photo to create a negative image.
- [Posterize](04-other-adjustments/04-adjustment-posterize.md)—expresses a photo as blocks of solid color.
- [Soft Proof](04-other-adjustments/05-adjustment-softproof.md)—provides a preview of a photo when output for a specific color space or device.
- [Normals](04-other-adjustments/03-adjustment-normals.md)—used to adjust and correct normal maps used in 3D game-art apps.
