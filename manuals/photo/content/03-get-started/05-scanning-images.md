# Scanning images

**macOS:**

You can scan printed images directly from your scanner into your project.

**Windows:**

For Windows, Affinity Photo 2 does not offer scanning directly from within the app. We recommend using your own scanner's software to acquire images.

**macOS:**

Your scanner needs to be correctly installed to allow it to be used. You can check the scanner is installed by choosing **Apple > System Settings (or Preferences)**, then choosing **Printers & Scanners**.

To scan an image:  Place the item you'd like to scan on the scanner bed. From the **File** menu, click **Acquire Image**. From the dialog, you can do one of the following: Click **Scan** for a scan without a low-resolution Overview scan as preview. Click **Show Details**, for an automatic Overview scan, then **Scan** for a full scan. Remember to set a higher **Resolution** (>300dpi) for your full scan.    Your scanned image appears as an 'Untitled' document.  If you're initially prompted to "Please select area to scan', enter the Show Details view. Drag a selection area to be scanned over the image.   The dialog remains open to allow you to continue scanning additional images.
